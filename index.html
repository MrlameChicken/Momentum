Your operating rules

  1. Plan: Pick one measurable outcome, define done, then select up to two smaller priorities.
  2. Execute: Protect one focus block. When resistance appears, use the five-minute restart.
  3. Verify: Run, test, inspect and explain. With AI-assisted code, don't equate generated code with validated work.
  4. Close: Record evidence and tomorrow's first command or action.
  5. Recover: Time with family, exercise and rest are planned parts of the system, not rewards for overwork.

The 14-day rollout

Days 1–3: One outcome, one focus block, one shutdown note.
Days 4–7: Repeat and notice friction.
Days 8–14: Change one thing based on your weekly review. Don't redesign the entire system.

Research foundations

  • Implementation intentions — Gollwitzer & Sheeran (2006): decide the cue and the response.
  • Goal-setting theory — Locke & Latham (2002): specific goals with feedback.
  • Procrastination as mood regulation — Sirois & Pychyl (2013): reduce the emotional cost of starting.
  • Practice testing — Dunlosky et al. (2013): retrieve and demonstrate understanding.
  • Habit formation — Lally et al. (2010): repetition in a consistent context.

The app's timers, scoring and daily limits are practical design choices, not clinically validated interventions.
